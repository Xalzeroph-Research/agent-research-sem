# SEM Evolution Authority v1

Status: implemented on the SEM project branch after the Minecraft live-provider P0 production closure.

## Authority split

SelfEvolve uses two separate authorities. Deterministic reflection admission decides only whether a fresh authoritative evidence cut may be inspected. The qualified Meta role owns the semantic `NO_EDIT` versus structural-edit decision.

Reflection admission requires a positive evidence sequence and admits a given `(generation, evidence_sequence, evidence_digest)` identity only once per session evolution controller. It does not require a minimum task dwell or `blocked_by_prior_progress` count.

RuleBasedEvolver remains the deterministic comparator. Its historical minimum-dwell and persistent-block thresholds are preserved in a separate rule-only eligibility policy rather than being reused as SelfEvolve authority.

## Scientific acceptance veto

Meta never approves its own proposal. Every typed edit still passes the paired branch evaluator from one comparable source cut and then a deterministic acceptance policy.

The candidate must be non-inferior on success rate and mean utility, and must not increase blocked-task or failed-task totals. Adoption additionally requires a strict improvement, beyond tolerance, in at least one predeclared outcome or efficiency dimension. Exact ties are rejected as no demonstrated benefit.

Lower step count or duration can justify adoption only after the core outcome and failure constraints are non-regressing. Memory-query count is descriptive and cannot by itself justify adoption.

Invalid comparability terminates before acceptance or adoption. Meta receives only the frozen architecture observation report and may cite only evidence refs in that report; candidate/control outcomes remain downstream of the Meta decision.
